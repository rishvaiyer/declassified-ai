Rulebooks of the Machines

Every AI chatbot is handed a hidden list of rules before it ever talks to you — who it is, what it must never say. Here are of them, from companies, pulled apart and laid side by side.

Source data: the CL4R1T4S archive, collected & maintained by Pliny · @elder-plinius. This project just visualizes his public collection.

Who Says No To What
